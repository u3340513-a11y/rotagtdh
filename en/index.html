About Us

We add value to the world's nutrition and to animal health

Since our founding in 2016, we have worked with the dream of producing sustainable, healthy and highly efficient solutions for the livestock industry. Today, in the field of animal feed additives, we are a reliable partner not only for producers in Turkey but for producers all around the world.

We develop our formulas with a rigor that stretches from the laboratory to the farm, always aiming to do better at every step. With our dynamic team and endless energy, we break the sector's conventional molds and deliver more innovative products every day.

  • Formulas developed through scientific R&D
  • Full compliance with international quality and safety standards
  • Transparent and dynamic business partnership approach
